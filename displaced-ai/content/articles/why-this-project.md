This is the first article on **Displaced AI**. Replace it with your own introduction.

A good opening post usually answers three questions:

1. **What are you looking at?** Name the people, industries or places this project follows.
2. **Why now?** Say what prompted you to start.
3. **What comes next?** Tell readers what kinds of articles and video to expect, and how often.

## Writing tips

Articles are plain Markdown files. You can use headings, **bold**, *italics*, [links](https://oecd.ai/), lists and quotes:

> Every article is one `.md` file in `content/articles`, plus one entry in `content/site.json`.

Images work too: upload them to the `content/images` folder and write `![description](content/images/photo.jpg)`.
