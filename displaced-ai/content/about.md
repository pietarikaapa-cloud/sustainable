**Displaced AI** is an independent project looking at what happens to people when artificial intelligence takes over parts of their work.

The site collects three kinds of material:

- **Articles**: original writing, interviews and field notes.
- **Video**: recorded conversations, talks and short documentaries.
- **Links**: the research, organisations and reporting this project draws on.

Edit this page in `content/about.md` to tell visitors who you are, why you started the project and how to get in touch.
